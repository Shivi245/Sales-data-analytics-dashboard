📌 Project Overview

    This project focuses on analyzing the sales performance data of a retail superstore to uncover revenue trends, profitability drivers, loss-making       
    segments, and actionable business insights.
    The analysis is presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard, along with documented insights to support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Business Problem

   Retail businesses often struggle to identify the following:

   Which regions and products drive profitability

   Which categories or sub-categories incur lossess

   How sales and profit trends change over time

   The objective of this project is to help stakeholders understand where to invest, optimize, and cut losses using data analytics.

🗂 Dataset Information

   Source: Superstore Sales Dataset

   Records: Sales transactions including order, customer, product, and regional details

   Key Columns:

   Order Date

   Region

   Category & Sub-Category

   Sales

   Profit

   Discount

   Quantity

📁 Raw and cleaned datasets were maintained separately to follow the industry’s best practices.

📊 Dashboard Overview

   The dashboard consists of the following sections.

1⃣ Executive Overview

   Overall sales & profit performance

   Monthly and yearly trends

   High-level KPIs

2⃣ Product Performance Analysis

   Category & sub-category wise sales and profit

   Identification of loss-making products

3⃣ Regional Performance

   Region and state-wise comparison

   Profitability analysis across locations

📸 Dashboard screenshots are available in the dashboard/dashboard_screenshots/ folder of the repository.

💡 Key Insights

   Certain regions contribute high sales but show lower profit margins due to heavy discounting practices.

   Specific subcategories consistently generate losses and require strategic intervention.

   A small set of products contributes significantly to the overall profit.
 
   Sales exhibit clear seasonal trends, indicating opportunities for targeted campaigns.

📌 Business Recommendations

   Optimizing discount strategies in low-margin regions.

   Focus marketing efforts on high-profit products and regions.

   Re-evaluate or discontinue consistently loss-making subcategories.

   Improving inventory planning based on demand and profitability trends.
