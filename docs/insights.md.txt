## Key Business Insights

- Technology category contributes the highest sales.
- Certain sub-categories show low profit despite high sales.
- West and East regions drive maximum revenue.
- Loss areas are visible clearly through profit-focused visuals.
- Interactive slicers enable multi-dimensional analysis by date, region, and category.

## Recommendations

- Review pricing strategy for low-profit segments.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing regions.
- Optimize product mix to reduce loss contribution.
